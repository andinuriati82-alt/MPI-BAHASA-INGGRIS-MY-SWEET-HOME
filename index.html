Setelah belajar, kamu dapat menyebutkan minimal 6 kosakata rumah, memahami prepositions of place, dan menulis 3–4 kalimat sederhana tentang rumah.

Prepositions of place — Kata depan tempat

📘⬆️🪑on
The book is on the table.
📘⬇️🪑under
The bag is under the table.
📘➡️🪑next to
The lamp is next to the bed.
📘📦in
The clothes are in the cupboard.
Sentence patterns
• This is my house.
• There is a sofa in the living room.
• There are two chairs in the dining room.
• The lamp is next to the bed.
Gunakan There is untuk satu benda dan There are untuk lebih dari satu.

6. Asesmen Akhir — Show What You Know

Kerjakan pertanyaan berikut untuk menunjukkan pemahamanmu.

A. Choose the correct answer.

1. There ___ two chairs in the dining room.

2. The refrigerator is usually in the ___.

B. Write 3–4 simple sentences about your home in English.

Rubrik sederhana untuk tulisan
• Kosakata rumah sesuai (0–2)
• Kalimat mudah dipahami (0–2)
• Penggunaan There is/There are atau prepositions tepat (0–2)
Nilai tulisan dapat dinilai guru dengan skor maksimal 6.
